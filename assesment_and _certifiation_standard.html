Assessment and Certification Standards

Set clear expectations about evidence, decisions and integrity.

Principle Methods Responsibilities Decisions Artificial intelligence Appeals

Assessment principle

Assessment should provide sufficient and appropriate evidence that a candidate has achieved the stated outcomes. The method should reflect the level and nature of the competence being assessed.

Possible assessment methods

Timed or untimed knowledge assessment

Scenario-based multiple-choice questions

Case analysis and written recommendations

Practical assignment using workplace tools or data

Presentation, interview or professional discussion

Workplace project or evidence portfolio

Capstone assessment integrating several competencies

Candidate responsibilities

01

Provide accurate identity and eligibility information.

02

Submit original work and acknowledge permitted sources and assistance.

03

Follow assessment instructions, confidentiality rules and deadlines.

04

Disclose conflicts, access needs or circumstances that may affect assessment.

05

Use the appeal route if a decision is believed to be procedurally or technically unsound.

Assessment decisions

Each programme should publish

  • Assessment components
  • Weighting
  • Pass standard
  • Permitted attempts
  • Resubmission conditions
  • Result timeline

Assessment evidence may be reviewed, moderated or sampled to support consistency.

Use of artificial intelligence

Candidates may use AI only within the rules stated for the assessment.

Where AI-assisted work is permitted, candidates remain responsible for

Accuracy

Originality

Confidentiality

Source acknowledgement

The final submission

Undisclosed or prohibited use may be treated as academic or professional misconduct.

Appeals

An appeal challenges an assessment decision or process; it is not simply a request for additional marks.

Appeals should identify

  • The decision
  • The reason for challenge
  • Supporting evidence

Appeals must be submitted within the published timeframe.
