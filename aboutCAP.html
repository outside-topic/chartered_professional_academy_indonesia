About CPA Indonesia

Practical professional development with a clear purpose

CPA Indonesia exists to help people and organizations build capability that can be applied, assessed and continuously improved.

Explore What We Offer

Who we are

Chartered Professional Academy Indonesia is a professional learning and membership organization focused on certification, corporate capability development and continuing professional growth.

We bring together structured learning, practical assessment, professional standards and development pathways so that learners can move beyond course attendance and demonstrate what they can understand, apply and improve.

What we do

Design and offer competency-based professional certification programmes.

Provide membership pathways that encourage ethical practice and continuous development.

Create customized corporate learning solutions linked to organizational priorities.

Offer short courses, masterclasses and executive development for individuals.

Work with approved partners, subject specialists and employers to maintain relevance.

Who we serve

Our programmes are intended for

  • Students preparing for professional life
  • Early-career employees
  • Specialists
  • Managers
  • Leaders
  • Entrepreneurs
  • HR and learning teams
  • Employers
  • Institutional partners

Our approach

  1. 01

    We begin with the capability people need.

  2. 02

    We define observable learning outcomes.

  3. 03

    We select an appropriate learning experience.

  4. 04

    We use assessment methods that match the intended competence.

  5. 05

    Where possible, we encourage assignments and projects based on authentic workplace challenges.

Our promise

We aim to

Communicate requirements clearly

Assess consistently

Protect learner information

Respond fairly

Improve our services using evidence and feedback
