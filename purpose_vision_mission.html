Purpose Vision Mission Values

Our purpose

To help professionals and organizations turn learning into demonstrated capability, stronger performance and responsible professional growth.

Our vision

To be a trusted Indonesia-based professional development community recognized for practical learning, fair assessment and meaningful career-long development.

Our mission

01

Develop relevant certification and learning pathways informed by workplace needs.

02

Use transparent standards and fit-for-purpose assessment to recognize applied competence.

03

Support members in maintaining ethical practice and continuous professional development.

04

Partner with employers, educators and practitioners to strengthen professional capability.

05

Make professional development accessible through flexible delivery and clear learner support.

Our values

What it means in practice

Integrity

We communicate honestly, protect assessment decisions and avoid claims we cannot support.

Relevance

We design learning around current responsibilities, career goals and organizational needs.

Application

We connect concepts with decisions, tools, projects and workplace evidence.

Fairness

We use clear requirements, consistent processes and accessible routes for review and appeal.

Continuous improvement

We use feedback, performance evidence and periodic review to improve our programmes.

Professional community

We encourage respectful exchange, shared learning and responsible contribution.

Chartered Professional Academy Indonesia supports professional growth through competency-based certification, membership, corporate learning and continuing professional development. Our programmes are designed to connect knowledge with practical performance in the workplace.
