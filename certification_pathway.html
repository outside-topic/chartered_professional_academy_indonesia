Certification pathway

A clear route from career goal to demonstrated competence

Understand each stage before you apply, including eligibility, learning, assessment, certification and continuing development.

Find a Certification

Certification pathway

  1. 01

    Define your goal

    Identify the role, capability or career move you want the certification to support.

  2. 02

    Choose the right programme and level

    Review the target audience, outcomes, recommended experience and progression opportunities.

  3. 03

    Check requirements

    Confirm documents, language needs, learning commitment, assessment method, fees and relevant policies.

  4. 04

    Apply or enroll

    Submit accurate information and complete the required payment or employer authorization.

  5. 05

    Complete the learning experience

    Participate in the required sessions, self-study, activities and learner support processes.

  6. 06

    Complete the assessment

    Submit or sit all required assessment components by the published deadline.

  7. 07

    Receive the decision

    Successful candidates receive the stated credential after results and quality checks are complete.

  8. 08

    Maintain your credential

    Where renewal applies, complete CPD, professional practice and renewal requirements within the stated cycle.

Recognition of prior learning

Where available, recognition of prior learning should evaluate relevant evidence against the same outcomes and standards required of other candidates.

Experience alone does not guarantee exemption or certification.

Applicable fees, evidence rules and appeal arrangements should be published separately.
