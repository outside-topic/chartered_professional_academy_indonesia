Certification Renewal and CPD

Learn how to maintain eligible CPA Indonesia professional certifications through continuing professional development and renewal requirements.

Keeping recognition current Renewal evidence CPD categories CPD quality Lapsed credentials

Keeping professional recognition current

Some credentials may remain valid indefinitely.

Others may require periodic renewal.

The applicable rule must appear on the individual certification page and certificate terms.

Typical renewal evidence

  • Completion of the required CPD hours or points

  • Relevant professional practice during the renewal cycle

  • Compliance with the code of professional conduct

  • Payment of the published renewal fee

  • Updated contact and employment information

  • Any required refresher, reassessment or declaration

Suggested CPD categories

01

Structured learning

courses, workshops, conferences and formal study

02

Work-based learning

projects, supervised practice and role expansion

03

Professional contribution

mentoring, speaking, writing and committee service

04

Self-directed learning

reading, research and reflective development

CPD quality

A useful CPD record explains

The activity

The capability developed

The learning gained

How it was applied

What changed as a result

Hours alone do not show professional value.

Lapsed credentials

If renewal requirements are not completed, the credential status may become expired, inactive or lapsed according to the published rules.

The reinstatement route may include

  • Outstanding CPD
  • An updated declaration
  • A fee
  • Reassessment
