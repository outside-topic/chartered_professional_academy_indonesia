How to Join

Follow the steps to apply for CPA Indonesia professional membership, submit evidence and maintain your member status.

Application steps Application evidence Decision Ready to apply

Application steps

  1. 01

    Review the membership categories and select the level that best matches your current profile.

  2. 02

    Check the eligibility criteria, evidence requirements, annual fee and member responsibilities.

  3. 03

    Create an account and complete the membership application accurately.

  4. 04

    Upload the required identity, education, certification, employment or reference evidence.

  5. 05

    Pay the published application or membership fee using an approved method.

  6. 06

    Respond to any verification questions from the membership team.

  7. 07

    Receive the decision and, if approved, activate your member profile and access benefits.

  8. 08

    Maintain your status through renewal, CPD and compliance with the code of professional conduct.

Application evidence

  • Government-issued identification

  • Current curriculum vitae or professional profile

  • Relevant qualification or certification evidence

  • Employment or experience confirmation where required

  • Professional references for senior categories

  • Signed professional conduct and information declarations

Decision statement

Meeting minimum requirements allows an application to be considered but does not guarantee admission.

CPA Indonesia may

  • Verify information
  • Request additional evidence
  • Decline an application

According to published criteria and procedures.

Ready to apply

Choose your membership category, prepare your evidence and create your application.
