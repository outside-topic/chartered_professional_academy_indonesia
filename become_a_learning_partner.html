Who may apply

Established training providers

Colleges or professional education institutions

Consulting and learning organizations

Employer academies

Other organizations with suitable governance, facilities and subject capability

What we assess

Legal identity and ownership

Governance and responsible officers

Financial and operational capacity

Premises, technology and learner support

Faculty competence

Marketing and admissions practices

Data protection and record management

Quality assurance and complaints capability

Conflicts, sanctions and reputational risk

Partner responsibilities

Approval should be programme-specific, time-limited and subject to written terms.

Partners must

  • Use current materials, approved claims and brand assets

  • Maintain records

  • Protect assessment integrity

  • Support learners

  • Cooperate with monitoring and corrective action

Application process

  1. 01

    Expression of interest

  2. 02

    Preliminary eligibility review

  3. 03

    Formal application and evidence

  4. 04

    Due diligence and capability review

  5. 05

    Interview or site review where required

  6. 06

    Decision and agreement

  7. 07

    Onboarding and programme approval

  8. 08

    Monitoring and renewal
