Professional certification designed around application

Knowledge matters when it improves decisions and performance. CPA Indonesia certifications are designed to connect learning with realistic work situations, structured assessment and practical evidence of competence.

Each programme page should clearly explain who the certification is for, what participants will learn, how they will be assessed, what evidence is required and how the credential is maintained.
