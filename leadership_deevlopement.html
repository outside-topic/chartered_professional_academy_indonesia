Leadership development

Develop leaders who turn direction into responsible action

Build practical leadership capability for new managers, experienced leaders and executives through role-relevant learning and application.

Measuring leadership development

Evaluation can examine

  • Confidence and knowledge
  • Observed behavior
  • Project delivery
  • Team practices
  • Agreed business indicators

Measures should be selected during design and interpreted with appropriate caution about attribution.
