What is a CPA Indonesia professional certification

A professional certification is a structured recognition pathway in a defined area of practice. It normally combines learning requirements with an assessment of knowledge, application or both.

The exact eligibility criteria, learning hours, assessment method, pass requirements, validity period and renewal conditions should be published on the relevant programme page.
