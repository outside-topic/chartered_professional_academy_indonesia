Quality and standards

Professional recognition must be supported by sound process

Our quality framework is intended to make programme design, assessment, credentialing and learner support consistent, transparent and open to improvement.

View Our Policies
Governance Quality cycle Assessment integrity Recognition Complaints

Governance principles

Clear authority and accountability for programme approval and review.

Separation of training support from final assessment decisions where practical.

Documented criteria for certification, membership and partner approval.

Conflict-of-interest declarations for assessors, reviewers and decision makers.

Secure records for learners, assessment outcomes, credentials and complaints.

Programme quality cycle

01

Need identification

Confirm the occupational or organizational capability being addressed.

02

Design

Define target audience, outcomes, content, learning hours and assessment method.

03

Technical review

Seek input from qualified practitioners and relevant stakeholders.

04

Approval

Authorize release only after requirements and resources are complete.

05

Delivery monitoring

Review attendance, learner support, trainer performance and assessment implementation.

06

Evaluation and revision

Use feedback, completion evidence, assessment performance and employer insight to improve the programme.

Assessment integrity

Assessment should test the stated outcomes. Depending on the certification, methods may include:

  • Knowledge tests
  • Case analysis
  • Applied assignments
  • Presentations
  • Workplace projects
  • Portfolios

Identity checks, originality controls, assessor guidance, moderation and an appeal route should be defined before enrolment opens.

Recognition and accreditation statement

CPA Indonesia will describe its registration, affiliations, approvals and external recognition only in the exact scope supported by valid documentation. Learners should review each programme page for its specific status.

Professional certification does not automatically confer an academic degree, government license or statutory right to practise.

Complaints and appeals

Learners and members should have access to published procedures for service complaints, assessment appeals, misconduct decisions and membership matters. Every submission should be acknowledged, reviewed by an appropriate person and answered within the published service standard.
