Customized training

Designed around your people and performance context

Adapt content, cases, tools, delivery and assessment to the capability your organization needs.

Request a Consultation
Process Client needs Boundaries Proposal information

Our customization process

01

Discover

Clarify strategy, performance expectations, stakeholder priorities and constraints.

02

Diagnose

Review target roles, current capability, evidence and root causes.

03

Design

Define outcomes, learning journey, practice, assessment and transfer support.

04

Develop

Create contextualized cases, participant resources and facilitator guidance.

05

Deliver

Facilitate through the agreed in-person, online or blended approach.

06

Evaluate

Review participation, learning, application and agreed performance indicators.

07

Improve

Recommend reinforcement, coaching, process changes or further development.

What we need from the client

Executive or functional sponsor

Clear target population

Access to relevant business context and subject stakeholders

Approved schedule and participant communication

Responsible handling of confidential information

Manager support for application after training

Customization boundaries

Customization should improve relevance without compromising assessment integrity, legal compliance, intellectual property rights or the stated standard of a credential.

Any client-specific certification conditions must be agreed before launch.
