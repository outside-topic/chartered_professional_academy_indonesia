How to choose

Start with the outcome you need, not the course title.

Review

  • The audience
  • Assumed knowledge
  • Learning outcomes
  • Practice method
  • Schedule
  • Evidence of completion

If a programme leads to certification, the assessment requirements should be shown separately.

Completion and recognition

A participation or completion certificate confirms attendance or successful completion of the stated learning requirements.

It should not be described as a professional certification unless a separate assessment and certification standard applies.
