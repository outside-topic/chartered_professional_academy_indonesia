Delivery and Evaluation Approach

Explain learning modes, facilitation standards and evidence of value.

Delivery options Facilitation standards Evaluation framework Reporting

Delivery options

Delivery modes with the situations they suit best and their design considerations
Mode Best suited to Design considerations
In person Practice, discussion, simulations and cohort connection Location, group size, travel, accessibility and room setup
Live online Distributed teams and facilitated virtual learning Platform access, session length, interaction and technical support
Self-paced Knowledge modules and flexible access Completion support, accessibility, assessment and content updates
Blended Extended journeys combining flexibility and application Sequence, learner communication, platform and reinforcement
Workplace project Application to a real operational or strategic issue Sponsor, scope, data access, confidentiality and review criteria

In person

Best suited to

Practice, discussion, simulations and cohort connection

Design considerations

Location, group size, travel, accessibility and room setup

Live online

Best suited to

Distributed teams and facilitated virtual learning

Design considerations

Self-paced

Best suited to

Design considerations

Blended

Best suited to

Extended journeys combining flexibility and application

Design considerations

Sequence, learner communication, platform and reinforcement

Workplace project

Best suited to

Application to a real operational or strategic issue

Design considerations

Sponsor, scope, data access, confidentiality and review criteria

Facilitation standards

Relevant subject and facilitation capability

Approved session plan and current materials

Inclusive and respectful learning environment

Accurate attendance and completion records

Protection of confidential examples and participant data

Structured feedback and continuous improvement

Evaluation framework

01

Participation and experience

02

Knowledge or skill development

03

Application and behavior

04

Operational or business indicators

05

Stakeholder interpretation and recommendations

Reporting

The agreed report may include

  • Participation
  • Completion
  • Assessment performance
  • Feedback themes
  • Application evidence
  • Sponsor observations
  • Recommended next steps

Business-impact claims should state the data source, timeframe and limitations.
