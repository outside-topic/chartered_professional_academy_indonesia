Designation and post-nominals

Any membership letters or designation must be formally approved, published and protected by clear usage rules.

Members may use only the designation attached to their current category and only while membership remains active and in good standing.

Upgrading membership

Members may apply for a higher category when they meet the published education, experience, responsibility and contribution criteria.

Upgrade is not automatic; additional evidence or review may be required.

Fees

Publish the application fee, annual subscription, taxes, renewal date, refund rule, late payment treatment and any reduced-rate eligibility in a separate fee schedule.

Avoid displaying fees that have not been formally approved.
