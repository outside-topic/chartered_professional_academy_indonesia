Continuing professional development

Plan learning that improves your practice

CPD is most useful when it begins with a real capability need and ends with reflection on what changed.

The CPD cycle

01

Reflect

Identify responsibilities, changes, risks and capability gaps.

02

Plan

Choose outcomes and activities relevant to your role and career direction.

03

Learn

Complete an appropriate mix of structured, work-based and self-directed activities.

04

Apply

Use the learning in decisions, projects, conversations or improved methods.

05

Record

Document the activity, evidence, learning and application.

06

Review

Evaluate progress and identify the next development priority.

What may count as CPD

Courses, workshops and professional events

Relevant certification and formal study

Applied projects and stretch assignments

Technical reading and structured research

Mentoring, coaching and supervised practice

Speaking, writing, teaching and professional contribution

Reflective review of significant professional experience

Suggested CPD record fields

01

Date and activity title

02

Development objective

03

Activity type and provider

04

Time or points

05

Key learning

06

Application to work

07

Evidence retained

08

Next action
